## Draw/create in code
- Rich dark purple background gradients, subtle texture/noise.
- ~5–7 glossy highly rounded soft-body blobs in yellow, pink, lilac, mint, blue, all with coherent 2.5D candy material.
- Gummy bubble letter glyphs generated from personalized name, **not** per-name PNG images.
- Tiny canvas particles, sparkles, confetti and collision bursts.
- HTML social icon controls and event popup card; icons can be recreated as original vector with legible labels (Facebook, Instagram, Website), never fake links.

## Constraints
- Do not import large decorative illustrations into every runtime scene. Optimize/crop/resize derivatives only where used.
- Do not turn scene into a flat cartoon; preserve glossy 2.5D candy look from 3-screen ref.
- For a light card, use logo with adequate contrast (original white on dark banner or a separately approved contrasting treatment; don't overwrite original).
- Keep decorative art from covering name, CTA or event info.
- Don't infer exact branding colors from sample without approval; initial colors are suggested in MASTER_SPEC.
- Fonts: body text requires robust Vietnamese support (e.g. Be Vietnam Pro). Gummy living letters require a fat, rounded *rendered contour* with custom highlights (validate Latin ASCII coverage). Fonts may be obtained as project dependencies according to their own licenses; don't deliver restricted font binaries.
